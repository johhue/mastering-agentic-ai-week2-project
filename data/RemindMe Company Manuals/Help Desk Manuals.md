## Description
This manual guides RemindMe help desk agents who support users, trusted contacts, and internal teams. RemindMe customers may contact support when an alarm did not fire, a social post was unexpected, a contact received a reminder, an event time was wrong, or a critical event was missed. The help desk must respond with speed, accuracy, privacy awareness, and empathy.

## Support Mission
The support mission is to help people trust RemindMe while being honest about what the service can and cannot do. Agents should reduce confusion, solve product problems, document failures, and escalate safety concerns. The best support interaction leaves the user clearer, calmer, and better prepared for their next reminder.

## Ticket Intake and Triage
Every ticket should include the account identifier, event name or approximate event time, affected channels, user device type if relevant, contact information involved, and the user's desired outcome. Do not ask for sensitive event details unless necessary.

Classify tickets by urgency. Critical tickets include potential safety risk, privacy exposure, unauthorized third-party contact, repeated delivery failures for active events, account takeover concerns, or messages suggesting immediate danger. Standard tickets include setup questions, billing issues, preference changes, and how-to requests.

## Common Support Categories
For phone alarm issues, check notification permissions, device operating system settings, quiet modes, app version, time zone, event schedule, acknowledgement status, and delivery logs.

For social media issues, verify that the user connected the correct account, approved the post, selected the intended audience, and understands that platform deletion may take time or may not remove shares and screenshots.

For friend, coworker, or relative outreach, confirm the selected contact, message timing, opt-out status, and whether escalation conditions were met. If the contact complains, help them opt out and route possible misuse to Trust and Safety.

## Privacy and Verification
Before discussing account details, verify the requester using approved authentication steps. Do not reveal event titles, contact names, phone numbers, social accounts, delivery logs, or private settings to an unverified person.

Trusted contacts may ask why they received a message. Agents may explain the general nature of RemindMe and provide opt-out help, but should avoid disclosing more event detail than the message already contained. If a contact reports harassment, take the report seriously and escalate.

## Escalation Procedures
Escalate privacy incidents, suspected account compromise, unauthorized social posting, abusive reminders, contact harassment, law enforcement requests, media inquiries, and legal threats to the appropriate team.

If a user or contact appears to be in immediate danger, follow the safety escalation procedure. Encourage the person to contact local emergency services. RemindMe support cannot dispatch emergency help.

Escalate technical delivery incidents when multiple users are affected, provider errors increase, queues are delayed, or monitoring indicates systemic failure. Include event IDs, timestamps, channels, provider responses, and screenshots when available.

## Tone, Scripts, and Boundaries
Use plain, calm language. Avoid blame. Do not say "the system definitely worked" unless logs and policy support that statement. Prefer: "I can review the delivery record and settings with you."

For a contact opt-out: "I can stop future RemindMe requests to this contact point. You do not need to explain why."

## Ticket Closure and Quality
Close tickets only after the issue is resolved, routed, or clearly explained. Summaries should include facts found, actions taken, user guidance, and any escalation. Quality reviews should check accuracy, empathy, privacy discipline, and correct use of tags.
