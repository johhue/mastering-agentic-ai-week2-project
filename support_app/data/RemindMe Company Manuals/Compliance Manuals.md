## Description
This manual defines the compliance expectations for RemindMe products and business operations. RemindMe helps users remember critical events by using phone alarms, social media prompts, friend or coworker outreach, and close-relative contact. These workflows create obligations around consent, privacy, consumer communications, platform rules, accessibility, safety, and truth in advertising.

## Compliance Principles
RemindMe follows five core compliance principles. First, users must understand what will happen before they activate a reminder. Second, contacts must not be misled about why they are being asked to help. Third, sensitive event information must be minimized and disclosed only when necessary. Fourth, reminder intensity must match the user's settings and applicable law. Fifth, incidents must be documented, investigated, and corrected.

## Consent and Contact Authorization
Before RemindMe contacts a friend, coworker, relative, or social media audience, the user must provide clear authorization through an approved product flow. The flow should identify the contact method, timing, message content, event sensitivity, frequency, and cancellation controls.

When a third-party contact is enrolled for repeated reminder support, RemindMe should provide a notice explaining who requested the reminder, what the contact may be asked to do, how to opt out, and how to report misuse. Consent records must be retained according to the records schedule. A contact's opt-out must be honored promptly.

## Privacy and Data Protection
RemindMe collects event titles, dates, times, notification preferences, contact details, delivery logs, account data, and support information. Some event descriptions may reveal health, financial, legal, religious, family, employment, or location information. Teams must practice data minimization and avoid requiring sensitive details when a neutral label will work.

Users must be given clear privacy notices and practical controls for correction, deletion, export, and preference changes where required. Requests involving personal data must be routed through approved privacy workflows.

## Communications Compliance
Phone alarms, texts, emails, push notifications, and social media posts may be subject to communications laws, carrier rules, platform policies, and anti-spam requirements. RemindMe must maintain opt-in and opt-out mechanisms for channels that require them. Marketing messages must be separated from transactional reminder messages.

## Safety, Abuse, and Misuse Controls
Compliance reviews must evaluate whether reminder escalation could create harm. Examples include notifying an abusive partner, revealing a medical appointment, pressuring a coworker into personal involvement, or sending repeated messages to someone who opted out.

High-risk features should include rate limits, audit trails, abuse reporting, user education, and the ability for Trust and Safety to suspend specific reminder paths. Physical-contact reminders must be framed as a request to a trusted person, not as an emergency dispatch service.

## Records, Audits, and Incidents
RemindMe must retain records needed to prove consent, investigate delivery failures, honor opt-outs, answer regulatory inquiries, and improve safety. Records must not be kept longer than necessary under the retention schedule.

## Training and Governance
Employees must complete compliance training during onboarding and annually thereafter. Product launches involving new reminder channels, new jurisdictions, minors, health-related workflows, or physical-contact escalation require compliance review before release. Exceptions to this manual must be documented and approved by Compliance and Legal.
