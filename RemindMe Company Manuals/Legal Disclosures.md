## Description
This document provides plain-language legal disclosures for RemindMe users, customers, partners, and internal teams. It is intended to support product notices, sales materials, onboarding flows, user agreements, and support responses.

RemindMe offers custom alarm and alerting tools that help people remember important events. The service can trigger phone alarms, send notifications, post user-approved social media reminders, ask friends or coworkers to call or text, and request that a close relative make personal contact. These tools are powerful, but they are not a substitute for emergency services, professional advice, or personal responsibility.

## Nature of the Service
RemindMe is a reminder and alerting software provider. We help users configure communication workflows. We do not guarantee that a person will wake up, read a message, answer a call, receive a post, respond to a request, travel to a location, or physically make contact.

The service depends on user settings, device status, internet access, carrier networks, platform availability, contact availability, notification permissions, battery levels, time zone accuracy, and third-party systems. Any of these factors may affect reminder delivery or effectiveness.

## No Guarantee of Reminder Delivery
RemindMe works to deliver reminders reliably, but no reminder system is perfect. Users should not rely on RemindMe as the only method for events where failure could cause death, bodily injury, loss of legal rights, financial loss, employment discipline, or other serious harm.

## User Responsibilities
Users are responsible for entering accurate event details, selecting appropriate reminder channels, obtaining any required permission from third-party contacts, and avoiding misuse. Users must not create reminders that harass, threaten, deceive, impersonate, shame, expose, or endanger another person.

## Third-Party Contact Disclosures
When a user asks RemindMe to involve a friend, coworker, or relative, that person may receive a message explaining that the user requested help with a reminder. The contact may decline, opt out, report misuse, or ignore the request. RemindMe does not control the contact's actions.

Users should select contacts who have agreed to help and who can respond appropriately. Coworker reminders should respect workplace policies and professional boundaries. Close-relative physical-contact reminders should be used only where the user has a trusted relationship and the request is safe, lawful, and reasonable.

## Social Media and Public Posting
Some RemindMe features may post or schedule user-approved social media messages. Public or semi-public posts can reveal personal information, invite comments, or be shared beyond the intended audience. Users should review platform settings and message content carefully.

## Emergency, Medical, and Legal Limits
RemindMe is not an emergency response provider. If there is an immediate danger, users or contacts should call local emergency services. RemindMe does not provide medical advice, legal advice, mental health services, transportation, caregiving, or law enforcement dispatch.

## Privacy, Data, and Updates
RemindMe collects and processes information needed to provide reminder services, manage accounts, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Details are described in the Privacy Policy and related notices.

These disclosures may change as RemindMe adds features, enters new markets, updates laws, or improves safety controls. Product teams must ensure user-facing language remains accurate and consistent with current service behavior.
