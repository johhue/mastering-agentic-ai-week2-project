## Description
RemindMe builds custom alarm and alerting software for people who cannot afford to miss critical events. Our work may involve sensitive life moments such as medical appointments, legal deadlines, medication reminders, travel departures, interviews, school events, caregiving duties, and personal safety check-ins. This HR policy explains how employees are expected to work with care, reliability, and respect while protecting users, colleagues, partners, and contacts who participate in reminders.

## Mission and Culture
Our mission is to ensure people never miss a critical event in their life. That mission requires judgment as much as technical skill. Employees should design, sell, support, and operate RemindMe with empathy for users who may be stressed, distracted, ill, elderly, neurodivergent, overloaded, or otherwise vulnerable.

## Employment Standards
RemindMe is an equal opportunity employer. Hiring, promotion, compensation, and termination decisions must be based on business needs, role requirements, performance, and lawful criteria. Discrimination, retaliation, harassment, bullying, intimidation, or exclusion based on protected status or personal identity is prohibited.

## Employee Conduct
Employees must communicate professionally with users, reminder contacts, coworkers, contractors, vendors, and regulators. We do not mock missed events, user anxiety, family dynamics, or accessibility needs. Internal jokes or labels that reduce users to "forgetful," "difficult," or "noncompliant" are not acceptable.

Because RemindMe workflows may involve social media posting, friend and coworker outreach, or relative contact, employees must treat all contact points as real people with their own boundaries. A reminder contact is not an employee of RemindMe and should not be pressured, shamed, or overused. Employees may not use customer information for personal reasons or contact users outside approved company systems.

## Privacy and Data Handling
Employees may access user data only when needed for assigned work. User events, reminder schedules, contact lists, phone numbers, social media handles, health-related clues, legal dates, or location information are confidential. Accessing celebrity accounts, friends, family, former partners, or coworkers out of curiosity is grounds for discipline, up to termination.

## Safety and Escalation Work
Some reminders may relate to safety-critical events. Employees must not promise emergency response, medical intervention, legal advice, or guaranteed physical contact unless a specific product, contract, and operating procedure provides it. When a user appears to be in immediate danger, employees should follow the emergency escalation procedure and involve trained personnel.

## Training and Accountability
All employees must complete onboarding training covering privacy, security, anti-harassment, accessibility, incident reporting, acceptable use, and customer empathy. Employees in support, trust and safety, engineering, compliance, and sales must complete additional training relevant to their systems and user interactions.

Managers are responsible for reinforcing these policies in daily work. Violations should be reported to a manager, People Operations, Legal, or the anonymous reporting channel. RemindMe prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Acknowledgement
Employees must read this manual, ask questions when expectations are unclear, and confirm that they understand their responsibilities. These policies may be updated as RemindMe grows, launches new reminder channels, enters new jurisdictions, or learns from incidents.
