## Description
This FAQ answers common questions about RemindMe, a custom alarm and alerting service designed to help people remember critical events. It can be used by customers, support agents, sales teams, and product reviewers who need consistent explanations of what RemindMe does.

## General Questions
### What is RemindMe?
RemindMe is software that helps you remember important events through phone alarms, notifications, social media prompts, and help from trusted people such as friends, coworkers, or relatives.

### Is RemindMe an emergency service?
No. RemindMe is not an emergency response provider. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## Reminder Setup
### How do I create a reminder?
Create an event, enter the date and time, choose the reminder channels, review the message content, and save the event. For important reminders, run a test first.

### What is escalation?
Escalation means RemindMe tries a stronger reminder method if earlier steps are not acknowledged or if you scheduled multiple reminder stages. For example, your phone alarm may come first, followed by a text request to a friend.

### Can I cancel an event?
Yes. You can cancel future reminders. If a message has already been sent or posted, cancellation may not remove what others have already received or seen.

## Trusted Contacts
### What is a trusted contact?
A trusted contact is someone you choose to help remind you. This might be a friend, coworker, roommate, partner, sibling, parent, adult child, or other close relative.

### Will my contact know why I added them?
They may receive a message explaining that you requested reminder help. You control the message content, but RemindMe may also provide basic information about opt-out and safety.

### Can a trusted contact opt out?
Yes. Contacts can decline or opt out of future requests. RemindMe expects users to respect that decision.

## Social Media Reminders
### Why would I use social media reminders?
Some people respond well to public or group accountability. A post asking others to remind you may help when private alarms are not enough.

### Can I approve the post before it goes out?
Yes. You should be able to preview and approve the content and destination before enabling a social media reminder.

## Privacy and Safety
### What data does RemindMe collect?
RemindMe collects information needed to provide the service, such as account details, event times, reminder settings, contact information, delivery status, and support records.

### Should I put medical or legal details in an event title?
Only if you are comfortable with the possible exposure. Neutral titles such as "Appointment" or "Deadline" are often safer.

## Troubleshooting
### My phone alarm did not sound. What should I check?
Check notification permissions, volume, silent mode, focus mode, battery settings, app version, time zone, and whether the event was saved correctly.

### My contact did not call me. Is that a product failure?
Not necessarily. RemindMe can request that a contact help, but the contact may be unavailable, unwilling, asleep, offline, or opted out.

### The time was wrong. What happened?
Time zone settings are a common cause. Check your account time zone, event time zone, travel settings, and daylight saving changes.

## Account and Billing
### Can I change my phone number or email?
Yes. Update your account settings and verify the new contact method before relying on it for reminders.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes, subject to retention rules for legal, security, billing, and abuse-prevention purposes.

### How do I get help?
Contact RemindMe support through the app or website. Include the event time, reminder channel, and what you expected to happen, but avoid sharing sensitive details unless needed.
